# Support

For help with cc-settings, or to report a bug, open an issue on GitHub.

[github.com/darkroomengineering/cc-settings/issues](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/cc-settings/issues)

It helps to include your operating system, whether you use Claude Code, Codex or both, the output of `npx darkroom-settings --status`, and what happened.
